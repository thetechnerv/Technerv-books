Install it on your phone

It installs like a normal app: its own icon, full screen, and a Tech Nerv launch screen. No App Store needed.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the app’s address in SafariThen sign in with your email code.
  2. Tap ShareThe square with an arrow, in the toolbar.
  3. Add to Home Screen → AddOpen it from the new icon from now on.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the address in ChromeA small “Install” prompt may appear on its own.
  2. ⋮ menu → Install appOr “Add to Home screen”.
  3. Long-press the iconShortcuts: Add expense, Snap a receipt, New invoice, Review.
In the installed appPull down from the top of any screen to refresh. If you’re offline you’ll see a clear banner; nothing financial is stored on the phone. After an update you’ll get “A new version is ready → Reload”. The same steps are inside the app under Settings → Install on your phone.

Your routine

The golden rules

1. Record it when it happens.

The receipt is in your hand right now; in three months it won’t be.

2. Always say how it was paid.

Business card, EQ Bank, or your personal card. This is what keeps the owner balances right.

3. Be honest about personal vs business.

Wrong card for groceries? Record it as Personal. It’s never deducted, and it shows what you owe the company.

4. Attach receipts to business expenses.

CRA can ask for them to support GST claimed back. Photos are compressed automatically.

5. Don’t delete issued invoices. Void them.

Editing a sent invoice keeps the old version with a reason. Your audit trail stays clean.

6. Import the bank monthly.

It’s the safety net that catches anything you forgot to record.

How to

Add an expense

  1. Tap + → Expense (or Snap receipt)On a computer press E.
  2. Type the totalSwitch CAD/USD if needed. USD converts at the Bank of Canada rate for that date.
  3. Add the receiptTake photo or Add file (PDFs too). Optional but strongly recommended.
  4. VendorStart typing; picking a past vendor fills in category, card, tax and project from last time.
  5. Who spent and Paid withYour avatar and the card or account used.
  6. What was it forBusiness, Personal, or Mixed with a business % slider. The green line underneath explains what will happen, e.g. “Business expense · company owes Deeparsh $42.10”.
  7. Sales taxPick the preset that matches the receipt (BC goods = GST 5% + PST 7%; services = GST 5%; Ontario = HST 13%; foreign = none). The amounts are worked back from the total; adjust if the receipt differs.
  8. SaveOr Save & add another when you’re catching up on a pile.
Extras on the formCategory (meals are automatically 50% deductible; equipment is treated as a capital asset), project, billable (re-bill it to a client later), tags and notes. The app warns you about likely duplicates.

Edit anytime: Expenses → tap it → Edit. You can also Duplicate, Delete, or Mark settled. List filters: No receipt, To settle, Personal, plus who / paid with / category / period / tag.

Business, personal or mixed: what happens

You record…Paid with the business card / EQPaid with your own card
BusinessA normal business expense.A business expense, and the company owes you the money back.
PersonalNot an expense. You owe the company (a shareholder loan), with a repay-by date.Not the company’s business; don’t record it.
Mixed (e.g. 60%)60% is a business expense; you owe the company the other 40%.60% is a business expense the company owes you; 40% is yours.
Shareholder-loan deadlinePersonal money taken through the company has to be repaid by the end of the following fiscal year (e.g. something from FY2026 by Sept 30, 2027), or CRA treats it as income. Owner balances shows the date for every open item and warns you in advance.

Create & send an invoice

  1. + → InvoiceOr on a client’s page: New invoice / Duplicate last. On a computer press I.
  2. Pick the clientTax and currency follow them: BC/AB → GST 5%, Ontario → HST 13%, Nova Scotia → HST 14%, US → zero-rated in USD.
  3. Add linesPick from Products & services or type your own: description, detail line, quantity, unit, price. Reorder with the arrows.
  4. Check dates & extrasDue date presets (on receipt / 7 / 15 / 30 days), PO number, project, discount (applied before tax), notes and terms.
  5. Save draft or Save & mark sent.
  6. Send it yourselvesOn the invoice: Share (phone: send the PDF by Mail, Messages, WhatsApp…) or Download PDF.

On an invoice you can also…

Copy reminder
A polite reminder with amount, due date and how to pay, ready to paste into an email. Records that you reminded them.
Edit after sending
It asks what changed and keeps the old version. Revisions lists every version, and any old PDF can be re-created.
Duplicate · Void · Back to draft · Credit note
Void instead of deleting. A credit note refunds or discounts after the fact.
Save PDF copy to records
Stores an exact copy of what you sent.
Recurring
Invoices → Recurring: monthly retainers, pause/resume, Create due drafts.

Invoice design: Settings → Branding & invoices (theme, colour, logo, footer, payment details) with a live preview.

Estimates → invoices

+ → Estimate works like an invoice, with a “valid until” date and an acceptance line on the PDF. Then: Mark sent → Accept or Decline → Convert to invoice (lines copied, linked both ways). Estimates shows the win rate.

Record a payment you received

  1. From the invoice: Record paymentOr + → Payment received, then choose the client.
  2. Check the allocationOne payment can cover several invoices; it fills the oldest first, and you can adjust each one.
  3. Method, date, account, referencee-Transfer reference, cheque number, etc. CAD goes into EQ Bank Business, USD into EQ USD.

Made a mistake? Payments → tap it → Edit or Delete; the invoice status updates itself. Bank deposits can also be turned into payments during bank review.

Import & review the bank

  1. Download a CSVEQ Bank online banking → account → download icon → CSV. Card: your card website’s transaction export.
  2. + → Import statementChoose the account, pick the file (works with the iPhone Files app), check the preview, import. You’ll see something like “128 new · 14 duplicates skipped”.
  3. ReviewOne transaction at a time with suggestions. Swipe right = accept the top suggestion, left = ignore. Or Match, Create expense, Record income (e.g. interest), Client payment, or Transfer (moving money between your own accounts, like paying the card).
  4. Make rules“Always do this for LINKEDIN…?” sets the category, vendor name and business/personal automatically next time. Manage them in More → Rules.
  5. ReconcileMonthly, enter the statement’s closing balance and aim for $0.00 difference.
Mistakes are reversibleEvery review action has Undo. A whole import can be undone from Banking → Import history.

Settle up between owners and the company

More → Owner balances shows, for each of you, whether the company owes you or you owe it, and why: out-of-pocket spending, personal charges, mileage, start-up loans, reimbursements, repayments.

  1. Tap Settle upPick the type: Reimbursement (company pays you), Repayment (you pay the company), Contribution, Dividend, Salary, Other.
  2. Choose what it coversThe amount defaults to what clears your open items. Offset only settles both directions without moving money.
  3. Send the actual e-TransferThe app records it; your bank moves it.

Log a trip

+ → Trip: date, from, to, purpose, km (toggle round trip), who, project. The CRA rate (73¢ per km for the first 5,000 km each year, 67¢ after) is applied automatically, and the trip adds to what the company owes you. Mileage → Reimburse pays it out; Export gives a CRA-style logbook.

Subscriptions

More → Subscriptions lists recurring tools (OpenAI, Google Workspace, Twilio…) with monthly and yearly cost in CAD and the next charge. Tap Log this month’s charge when one bills; you’ll get a badge if the price changed. Pause one with its switch.

Keep documents

More → Documents is the company filing cabinet, by fiscal year: incorporation papers, GST registration, filed returns, notices of assessment, contracts, insurance, monthly statements. Add with Upload; set an expiry date to get a reminder. The Statements tracker shows which months are missing for each account; tap + to upload that month.

File the GST/HST return (annually, due Dec 31)

  1. Tax Centre → GST/HST returnChoose the fiscal year (it defaults to the one that just ended).
  2. Clear the checksExpenses claiming GST with no receipt, foreign vendors that charged GST, unusual tax codes.
  3. Copy each line into CRAEvery GST34 line (101, 103, 106, 109…) has a Copy button. Type in anything that’s yours to fill (instalments paid, adjustments).
  4. Mark as filedDate, confirmation number, amount and paid date. Attach the CRA confirmation; it goes into Documents.
InstalmentsIf the net tax is $3,000 or more, CRA expects quarterly instalments the following year. The worksheet tells you when this applies.

Year-end for the accountant (T2)

Tax Centre → T2 year-end package shows the income statement grouped the way CRA wants it (GIFI codes), meals add-backs, capital assets with a depreciation (CCA) estimate, shareholder balances, receivables and bank balances at Sept 30. Download package gives one zip with everything, including every receipt. Send it to your accountant.

Close the booksOnce the return is filed, Settings → Tax → Close the books locks that year so nothing changes by accident. It can be reopened.

Glossary

ITCInput tax credit: GST/HST you paid on business purchases and claim back on your return.
Zero-ratedTaxable at 0%, e.g. services exported to a US client. You still report the sale.
Place of supplyThe client’s province decides GST (5%) vs HST (13–15%).
GIFICRA’s standard codes for financial statements on the T2 (e.g. 8523 meals).
CCACapital cost allowance: tax depreciation on equipment such as computers (class 50) and furniture (class 8).
Shareholder loanMoney the company paid for an owner’s personal things. Repay it in time or it becomes income.
ReconcileConfirm the app’s balance matches the bank statement for a month.
Fiscal year (FY)Named for the year it ends: FY2026 = Oct 1, 2025 – Sep 30, 2026.

FAQ

Does the app email invoices to clients?

No, by design. Download or Share the PDF and send it yourselves from Mail, Gmail or Messages, then tap Mark as sent. Copy reminder gives you ready-made text.

I paid for a business thing with my personal card. What do I do?

Add the expense as Business with Paid with = your personal account. The company now owes you; settle up later from Owner balances.

I used the business card for something personal.

Add it as Personal with Paid with = Business Card. It isn’t deducted and shows as money you owe the company. Repay it by the date shown.

The phone bill is partly business. How?

Choose Mixed and set the business %. Only that share is deducted and only that share’s GST is claimed.

Can I bill a client for an expense?

Turn on Billable on the expense with a project. The project page lists unbilled expenses, and “Invoice these” creates the invoice lines.

What if I import the same bank file twice?

Nothing happens. Duplicates are detected and skipped. You can also undo a whole import.

A client paid in USD. Is that OK?

Yes. USD clients are invoiced in USD (zero-rated); payments record the exchange rate, and reports convert to CAD.

Is my data stored on the phone?

No. Your books stay in your private, secured database. The installed app only keeps its own icons and code for speed. Signing in uses a one-time code emailed to you, and only the two of you can sign in.

How do we start for real?

When you’re happy with the demo, ask for a fresh start. All sample records and files are removed; your settings, members, accounts, categories and tax rates stay. Then update Company details and import your real statements.
